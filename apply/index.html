Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this program for?

The Nourished Woman Method is designed for women who want a complete transformation in their relationship with food, body, and daily habits. It's ideal for those who are tired of quick fixes and ready for lasting, meaningful change grounded in science and self-compassion.

What's the time commitment?

Plan for 3-4 hours per week: one live coaching session plus implementation of your personalized plan. The program is designed to fit into a busy lifestyle, and Ana will work with you to create a plan that's sustainable for your schedule.

Is this program right for me if I have dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Every plan is fully personalized to your needs, allergies, and preferences. Whether you're vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, or managing a specific condition, Ana will tailor everything to work for you.

What happens after I apply?

Ana personally reviews every application. If it's a good fit, you'll be invited to a free 15-minute discovery call to discuss your goals, answer any questions, and make sure the program is the right next step for you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer 3 monthly payments of $899 as an alternative to the full $2,497 investment. Payment plan details will be discussed during your discovery call so we can find the option that works best for you.
